PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Foldera is a paper-folding puzzle. The player drags across the board, the
sheet folds along that line, and every tile mirrors at once; matching pairs
meet and disappear. It ships with 500 hand-checked levels. The audience is
casual puzzle players of any age who want a calm game with no timers, no
lives and no pressure. Every level is machine-verified before release, so a
board can never become unsolvable no matter what order the player folds in --
that is the problem the game solves and the reason people stay with it.

HOW TO SET UP AND ACCESS EVERY FEATURE
There is nothing to set up. Launch the app and the first level starts. There
is no account, no login, no registration and no configuration screen, so no
credentials are needed to review the app. All 500 levels are on the device
and the entire app works offline, including in airplane mode. Later rules
(pinned tiles, jokers, shrinking paper) are introduced automatically as the
player progresses; no unlock, purchase or code is required to reach them.
Themes and settings are reachable from the title screen.

EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for advertising is the ONLY external
service the app contacts. There is no backend of our own, no authentication
service, no payment processor, no AI service and no third-party analytics
SDK. In-game event logging exists but is written to a local file on the
device and is never transmitted. The app is built with Godot Engine 4.7.

IN-APP PURCHASES AND ACCOUNTS
There are no in-app purchases on iOS. The purchase layer in the codebase is
Google Play Billing only and is disabled on this platform, so no purchase or
restore controls are shown. There is no account creation, therefore no
account deletion flow is required. There is no user-generated content, no
chat and no social feature, therefore no content reporting or blocking
mechanism is required. Age rating is 4+.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app behaves identically in every region. It is localized into 13
languages (text only; the puzzles, rules and content are the same
everywhere). Advertising is non-personalised in all regions: the app does not
present an App Tracking Transparency prompt and requests non-personalised ads
only, which is why the App Privacy labels declare no tracking.

REGULATED INDUSTRY OR PROTECTED THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app is a self-contained puzzle game in no regulated
industry. All artwork, audio, level data and source code are original work by
the developer; the app uses no third-party content, which matches the content
rights declaration on this listing.
